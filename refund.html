Refund Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Strict No Refund Policy

At Hrimkar Astro, all payments are final and non-refundable once a consultation is booked. By proceeding with a booking, you acknowledge and agree to this policy.

No Refunds Under Any Circumstances

Once payment is made for any consultation or service at Hrimkar Astro:

  • No refunds will be provided under any circumstances
  • This applies to all services including:
    • Vedic Astrology Consultations
    • Marriage Astrology
    • Career Astrology
    • Medical/Health Astrology
    • Numerology
    • Numerology + Astro Combo
    • Tarot Card Reading
  • No partial refunds are available
  • No credit notes will be issued in lieu of refund

Why No Refunds?

Our astrologers dedicate significant time and effort to prepare for each consultation. This includes:

  • Pre-consultation preparation: Detailed analysis of birth charts before your session
  • Time allocation: Exclusive time blocked for your consultation that cannot be given to other clients
  • Personalized research: Individual study based on your specific birth details and questions
  • Resource investment: Preparation of charts, calculations, and remedial suggestions

Important: Due to the personalized nature of astrological consultations, once a booking is confirmed and preparation begins, the service is considered rendered in part.

Rescheduling Available

While we do not offer refunds, we understand that circumstances may change. Therefore, we offer a one-time rescheduling option:

  • Request rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance
  • New appointment must be within 15 days of original booking
  • Only one reschedule allowed per booking
  • No-shows will forfeit the consultation entirely with no rescheduling option

No-Show Policy

If you fail to attend your scheduled consultation without prior notice:

  • The consultation will be marked as completed
  • No refund will be provided
  • No rescheduling will be allowed
  • You will need to book and pay for a new consultation

Cancellation by Hrimkar Astro

In the rare event that we need to cancel your appointment (due to astrologer unavailability or unforeseen circumstances):

  • We will offer an alternative time slot at your convenience
  • If no suitable alternative is available, a full refund will be provided
  • This is the only exception to our no-refund policy

By Booking, You Agree

By completing a booking on Hrimkar Astro, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You have read and understood this No Refund Policy
  • All payments are final and non-refundable
  • You will attend your scheduled consultation or request rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance
  • No-shows will result in forfeiture of the consultation

Questions About Our Policy?

If you have any questions about our refund policy or need to reschedule your appointment, please contact us.
