Frequently Asked Questions

What information do I need to provide for a consultation?

For an accurate horoscope reading, we need your exact date of birth, time of birth (as precise as possible), and place of birth. If you don't know your exact birth time, we can still provide insights using alternative methods.

How do online consultations work?

Online consultations are conducted via phone call or WhatsApp chat. After booking, you'll receive a confirmation email with the details. We will call you at your scheduled time.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time through your dashboard or by contacting us directly. Rescheduling within 24 hours may incur a small fee.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a full refund if you cancel more than 48 hours before your appointment. Cancellations within 24-48 hours receive a 50% refund. Please see our Refund Policy for complete details.

Are the remedies you suggest safe to follow?

Yes, all remedies we suggest are traditional Vedic remedies that are safe and time-tested. We recommend mantras, gemstones, donations, and specific rituals based on authentic scriptures. We never suggest anything harmful or unethical.

How long does it take to receive my detailed report?

Written reports are typically delivered within 5-7 business days after your consultation. Premium detailed reports may take up to 10 days as they include extensive analysis and personalized recommendations.
