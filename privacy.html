1. Introduction

Hrimkar Astro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our astrology consultation services.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register for an account
  • Book a consultation
  • Fill out a contact form
  • Subscribe to our newsletter

This information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date, time, and place of birth (required for astrological analysis)
  • Payment information (processed securely through payment partners)

2.2 Astrological Data

For the purpose of providing accurate astrological services, we collect:

  • Birth details (date, time, location)
  • Questions and concerns you share during consultations
  • Consultation recordings (with your consent)
  • Generated birth charts and reports

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you access our website, we automatically collect certain information:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: To deliver astrological consultations, generate birth charts, and provide personalized readings
  • Communication: To send appointment reminders, consultation reports, and respond to your inquiries
  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Payment Processing: To process transactions for our services
  • Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our services
  • Marketing: To send promotional content (only with your consent)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Payment Processors: We share payment information with secure payment gateways to process transactions
  • Service Providers: We may share information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website and services
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights
  • With Your Consent: We may share information with your explicit consent

Important: Your birth details and consultation content are treated with the highest confidentiality and are never shared with third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure servers with regular security updates
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive astrological data

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality and user authentication
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our website.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
